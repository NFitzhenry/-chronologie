Who is playing?

Choose a nickname and a 4-digit PIN. Use the same pair on any device to carry on with your scores and collection. A new nickname is created the first time it is used.

What is stored: your nickname, a scrambled copy of your PIN, and your game record (points, cards collected and results). Nicknames and scores appear on the public leaderboard. Please use a nickname rather than your real name.
